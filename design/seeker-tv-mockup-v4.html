v4 draft: v3 plus three system widgets under the weather (CPU, MEMORY, STORAGE; shown only when Settings > STATS FOR NERDS is ON), Apple-style cards with a ring gauge that turns red at 90%. Values here are simulated (Widgets buttons); the TV reads the real ones every 2 s (storage every 30 s). Click the screen, then ↑ ↓ to spin, Enter to open, hold Enter (0.6 s) for reorder mode (↑ ↓ moves the app, Enter drops it). → jumps to the settings gear, ← comes back. Weather and temperature are sample values; the real launcher pulls the WEATHER LOCATION setting from Open-Meteo. On the TV, the launch screen also shows the app's own icon.
