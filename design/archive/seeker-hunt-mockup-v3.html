Playable mockup at 1920x1080, 8-bit style (square pixels, flat palette). Enter is the remote's OK button. The sight locks onto a bird on its own; ← → jump to the next bird left or right, and the dog stays put but turns to face whatever you're aiming at. About one shot in four misses. Hit a bird and that service "opens". The dog, ducks, scene and sounds are our own designs in the era's style.
