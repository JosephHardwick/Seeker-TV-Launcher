Playable mockup at 1920x1080. Enter is the remote's OK button. Crash into a tube and that service "opens" (brand takeover), then the game returns to START. Hit the floor or ceiling and it picks the next tube ahead of you. Tubes cycle through all eight services in a fresh random order before any repeats. The bird and tubes are our own dot-matrix drawings. The rainbow strip along the top is there to drive the TV's bias lighting; it runs calm on START and full brightness in play.
