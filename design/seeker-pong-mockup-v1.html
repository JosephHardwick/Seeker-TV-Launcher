Playable mockup at 1920x1080. Enter is the remote's OK button. The beam of light on the right is a streaming service in its brand color. Every time you return the ball, the beam moves on to the next service (NEXT shows which). Miss the ball and the service on the beam "opens" (brand takeover), then it's back to START. SETTINGS is in the cycle too, as a gray beam. The CPU never misses, so the only way a point ends is you letting the ball past. Sound is synthesized in the page; the TV build uses the same waveforms.
