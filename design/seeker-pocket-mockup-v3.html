Pocket v3: proportions from the handheld (big message box, 52-px tiles, a tall START frame on the selection, scrollbar band edge to edge), pixel-true. The whole screen is 384x216 pixels (a handheld's 256x192 widened to 16:9) shown at x5, so nothing is smooth: text, frames, brackets, icons and motion all move in whole pixels. Click the screen, ← → to move, Enter to open, hold Enter for MOVE / HIDE / CANCEL. On the TV each tile is the app's own icon shrunk to 32x32 pixels; here they're stand-ins made the same way.
